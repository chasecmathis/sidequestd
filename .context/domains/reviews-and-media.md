# Reviews and media

## Review rules

- **One review per user per game.** The constraint `uq_reviews_user_id_game_id`
  enforces this, and the service checks first so the client gets a 409
  (`ReviewAlreadyExistsError`) that points at editing. A client finds the
  review to edit with `GET /users/{id}/reviews?game={gameId}`.
- **The game is not editable.** Pointing a review at a different game would
  silently change what everyone who liked or commented on it was responding
  to. To change the game, delete the review and write a new one.
- `PATCH` moves `updated_at`. `DELETE` also removes the stored media objects.
- `viewer_has_liked` is false when the caller is signed out.
- **Rating:** stored as an integer from 1 to 10 and shown as 0.5–5.0 stars. Both
  `rating` and `stars` are on the wire, so the clients never round
  differently from each other.
- Text is at most 5000 characters. `playtime_minutes` is optional and
  self-reported; clients edit it in hours.
- **Reviews inherit their author's privacy.** Every read goes through
  `users.require_content_access`. Reads are open to signed-out callers, and
  only the owner can write.
- Each review write recomputes `games.rating_average` and `rating_count`
  (`refresh_game_rating`) in the same transaction.
- Creating a review notifies every ACCEPTED follower of the author who has that
  game on their To Be Played or Playing list (`BACKLOG_GAME_REVIEWED`; recipients
  come from `notifications.backlog_reviewers_of`).
- Likes and comments are *counted* here through `interaction_stats` and
  *written* in [Interactions](interactions.md).

## Media pipeline

- **Upload is synchronous and cheap.** The type is sniffed from the file's
  leading bytes, and the declared Content-Type is ignored. The file is checked
  against an `UploadPolicy`: images up to 15 MB, clips up to 100 MB, avatars up
  to 5 MB. A clip's duration is read from its container headers, and anything
  longer than 60 s is refused immediately. A review holds at most 10 media
  items and only one clip. Storage keys are generated by the server.
- **Metadata (EXIF/GPS, `udta`/`meta`) is stripped before the file reaches the
  bucket** on both the review and avatar paths. The privacy policy promises
  this, so it must stay true.
- **Only MP4 and QuickTime clips are accepted.** WebM is refused, because
  measuring its duration needs an EBML parser, and a clip that can't be
  measured can't be held to the 60 s cap.
- **Processing is asynchronous.** It records dimensions, renders a 640px
  thumbnail, and moves the item PENDING → READY or FAILED. It runs as a FastAPI
  background task after the upload. `app.cli.process_media` is the catch-up
  sweep, and it is idempotent because it only advances rows out of PENDING.
- **It runs offline:** Pillow for images, and a clip's length is read straight
  from the ISO-BMFF container (`moov` → `mvhd`). There is no ffmpeg, so clips
  are **not transcoded** and get **no poster frame**. `thumbnail_url` stays
  null for clips, and grids fall back to the game's cover art.
- Clients poll a review's detail while any media item is still pending.
- Deleting a media item deletes its objects and renumbers the carousel.

## Endpoints

`POST /reviews` · `GET|PATCH|DELETE /reviews/{review_id}` ·
`POST /reviews/{review_id}/media` · `DELETE /reviews/{review_id}/media/{media_id}`
